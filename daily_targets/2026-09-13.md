# Coffee Chat Targets -- 2026-09-13

**LinkedIn Premium workaround:** you don't need Premium to send a connection request -- you're hitting LinkedIn's free-tier limit of ~5 *personalized notes* per week. Once that's used up, either (a) send a blank (no-note) request to the rest -- still free -- or (b) message people with "Open Profile" enabled, which is free for anyone. Once someone accepts your connection, all further messages to them are free regardless of note limits.

**Change starting today: no more guessed email addresses.** Constructing likely personal emails for named individuals from a company's email-format pattern and publishing them to this repo crossed into building a persistent, shareable database of real people's probable contact info without their consent -- too close to the scraping problem this tool was built to avoid, just one step removed. If email is your only channel for someone, look them up yourself via a tool you control (with its own compliance posture), or use LinkedIn/the alumni workflow instead. Entries below have a source but no email guess.

These are Director/VP-level people (not C-suite) for better reply odds.

**Note on BCG:** third time's the charm -- found a current, correctly-leveled person today (Nick Cristea, Associate Director) via a 2025 BCG publication. The previous two days' failures suggest BCG's public bios/press just skew senior; searching recent BCG *publications* (co-authored insight pieces) rather than people/leadership pages worked better here.
